# Scientific Source Register

This register identifies the source that can support each class of public claim. Retrieval dates matter for FamilyTreeDNA pages because branch dates, variants, and tester counts can change.

## Private Primary Inputs

The ignored local directory `ftdna/simon_ellefsen/` contains the private FamilyTreeDNA exports used for the 2026-09-02 aggregate audit:

- an autosomal Family Finder genotype CSV;
- a full mitochondrial FASTA;
- Big Y VCF and callable-region BED data;
- FamilyTreeDNA result captures used to confirm displayed ancestry categories.

**Boundary:** these are `verified-private` inputs. Their row-level contents, sequences, kit identifiers, filenames, and match information are not public sources and must not enter Git. Only the approved aggregates in the [dated audit](project-audit-2026-09-02.md) may be cited publicly.

## FamilyTreeDNA Methodology and Help

### myOrigins v3 White Paper

- Source: [FamilyTreeDNA, myOrigins Version 3 White Paper](https://blog.familytreedna.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/myOrigins_3_WhitePaper.pdf)
- Supports: vendor methodology, reference-population framing, and caution around small components.
- Does not support: treating an estimate as nationality, a genealogical ancestor count, or an exact family-tree location.

### myOrigins Introduction

- Source: [FamilyTreeDNA Help Center, myOrigins Introduction](https://help.familytreedna.com/hc/en-us/articles/360004683915-myOrigins-Introduction)
- Supports: product-level explanation of populations and result presentation.
- Status: mutable vendor documentation; record a retrieval date when quoted.

### Notable Connections Help

- Source: [FamilyTreeDNA Help Center, mtDNA Discover Notable Connections](https://help.familytreedna.com/hc/en-us/articles/12740719271567-Using-the-mtDNA-Discover-Notable-Connections-Report)
- Supports: the warning that notable connections are not necessarily direct ancestors and may share ancestors thousands of years ago.
- Presentation rule: keep these separate from peer-reviewed ancient-DNA connections.

## Peer-Reviewed Ancient-DNA Context

### Danish Population History

- Source: [Allentoft et al., “100 ancient genomes show repeated population turnovers in Neolithic Denmark,” Nature 2024](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-023-06862-3)
- Supports: broad ancient Danish population context and specific published ancient samples.
- Does not support: identifying a sampled ancient individual as Simon's direct ancestor.

### Bell Beaker Genomic Transformation

- Source: [Olalde et al., “The Beaker phenomenon and the genomic transformation of northwest Europe,” Nature 2018](https://www.nature.com/articles/nature25738)
- Supports: broad Bell Beaker migration and ancestry context relevant to western European R1b lineages.
- Does not support: assigning the terminal R-BY67151 branch to a specific prehistoric migration or person.

## Citation Policy

- Vendor sources describe vendor methods and tree results.
- Reference records support sequence nomenclature.
- Peer-reviewed papers support population-level and ancient-sample context.
- Private exports support only locally verified aggregates.
- Narrative claims must not outrun the narrowest source in their chain.
