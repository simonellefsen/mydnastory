# Retire v2, v3, and v4

## Context

After reviewing the deployed alternatives, the original v1 page was judged clearer and more effective for this project.

## Decision

- Keep the original localized v1 routes as the only active product.
- Remove v2/v3/v4 routes, navigation, evidence presentation code, version-only tests, and version-only dependencies.
- Keep the wiki and Git branches as historical records, not as active routes.
- Preserve the private-data deployment exclusion and the original page's existing content.

## Outcome

The rollback was implemented on `main` and redeployed on 2026-09-02. A new redesign requires a fresh product decision.
